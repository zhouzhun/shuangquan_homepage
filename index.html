Shuang Quan, Ph.D.

Assistant Professor of Education

Director of Artificial Intelligence and Literacy Education Lab

Dr. Shuang Quan is an Assistant Professor of Education at Juniata College and the Director of the Artificial Intelligence and Literacy Education (AILE) Lab. She received her Ph.D. in Innovation in Curriculum and Instruction from Fordham University’s Graduate School of Education, with a professional background in Literacy Education and Early Childhood Education. Through the AILE Lab, Dr. Quan leads interdisciplinary research examining how emerging technologies, particularly AI-powered conversational learning systems, can support children’s literacy, language, cognitive, and social-emotional development across diverse learning environments. As a Society for Research in Child Development (SRCD) Early Career Scholar and an Early Research Innovation Network (ERIN) Fellow at Columbia University, Dr. Quan’s research has been supported by competitive grants and recognized through peer-reviewed publications and presentations at national and international conferences.

Dr. Shuang Quan is also committed to preparing future educators for AI-enhanced classrooms. Drawing on her 10 years of experience as an elementary school teacher, she develops curriculum and learning experiences that help preservice teachers build AI literacy, understand responsible AI practices, and integrate emerging technologies to create meaningful learning experiences in contemporary classrooms.

Artificial Intelligence and Literacy Education (AILE) Lab

Launched in December 2024, the AILE Lab investigates how artificial intelligence can transform children’s literacy learning. Bringing together talented student researchers from Education and Computer Science, the AILE Lab collaboratively designs, develops, and implements AI-powered tools that support the language and cognitive development of young children from diverse linguistic and socioeconomic backgrounds.

Early Literacy Development

Developing innovative approaches to support language and literacy development in early childhood education settings.

Educator Preparation

Preparing future educators to integrate AI technologies effectively and ethically in their teaching practice.

AILE team members facilitated the Language & AI Panel at the EAP 30th Anniversary Celebration, held at the Statton Learning Center at Juniata, April 2025.
